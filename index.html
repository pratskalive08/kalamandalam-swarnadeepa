About

The art of dance finds the one who loves it...

...and the same happened with Kalamandalam Swarnadeepa, who was initiated into dance at the age of three. Beginning with Rabindra Nritya, she later added Kathak, Bharatanatyam and Nazrul Nritya before the emotive nature of Mohiniyattam caught her imagination. Kerala Kalamandalam, the cradle of Indian Arts, welcomed her with open arms.

Gurus & Training

Kalamandalam Swarnadeepa received her Diploma in Mohiniyattam and Kuchipudi from Kerala Kalamandalam Deemed University under renowned gurus. A National Scholarship awardee, she continues her practice under the guidance of Dr. Guru Smt. Thankamani Kutty and Pallavi Krishnan.

Awards & Recognition

Kalamandalam Swarnadeepa is a graded artist of Doordarshan Kendra, Kolkata. She was recognised for her unique non-stop 26-hour performance in 2012 at Rabindra Bharati, Hyderabad, and received a National Scholarship from the Government of India for higher education in Mohiniyattam and Kuchipudi.

Her honours also include the Best Durga title, Best Young Talent at the Dover Lane Music & Dance Competition, and selection for the West Bengal best dancer awards.

Choreographies

Kalamandalam Swarnadeepa has performed for prestigious platforms at home and abroad.

Her work has travelled across Europe, the United Kingdom, Russia and Qatar, showcasing and conducting workshops in Mohiniyattam and Kuchipudi.

Nrityabritti BATAYAN

Kalamandalam Swarnadeepa is an ardent proponent of Indian classical arts, namely Mohiniyattam and Kuchipudi. She spends a significant part of her time teaching and promoting her craft at Nrityabritti Batayan, an institute established by her mother Itushree Mahanta.

Established in 1996, the academy has nurtured young talent into artists with finesse for over two decades.

Today the academy occupies a prominent place in Durgapur as an institution for Mohiniyattam, Kuchipudi and Bharatanatyam. It also teaches Creative Dance, Rabindra and Nazrul Nritya, recitation, music, instruments and vocal training.

It operates in Durgapur and Kolkata. Find out more at www.nrityabrittibatayan.com.

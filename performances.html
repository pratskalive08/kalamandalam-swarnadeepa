Performances &
Choreographies

She has performed in Germany in 2008–2009 and from 2010 onward, presenting classical dance across Berlin, Frankfurt, Stuttgart, Leonberg, Köln and Sweden. She has also performed at the Indian Embassy in Berlin and conducted dance workshops there.

In 2017 she performed and conducted a Mohiniyattam workshop in Russia. In 2019 she travelled to Bharat Utsav, Doha, Qatar, for a classical group performance organised by ICC and a workshop organised by the Embassy of India.

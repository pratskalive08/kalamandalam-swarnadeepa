The Full Story

Background

Born into a family with a long lineage of traditional performing arts, it was natural for Swarnadeepa to take to the classical arts. Initiated into dance at an early age, she was gradually drawn towards Mohiniyattam through the artistic legacy of her mother and Guru T. Shankaranarayanan. It is this legacy and commitment to Mohiniyattam that Swarnadeepa has imbibed.

Gurus & Training

1987–1990

Kathak under Guru Manisha Bhattacharya

1990–2000

Bharatanatyam under Guru Kalamandalam T. Shankara Narayanan, Principal, Sangeet Bhavan, Viswa Bharati, Shantiniketan

2000–2004

Mohiniyattam under Guru Kalamandalam Pushpalata

2004–2007

Kuchipudi under Guru Kalamandalam Rajalakshmi

2002–2006

Diploma in Kuchipudi and Mohiniyattam from Kerala Kalamandalam Deemed University, under Gurus Kalamandalam Padmini, Kalamandalam Leelamma, Kalamandalam Hymavathi, Kalamandalam Pushpalata and Kalamandalam Rajalekshmi

Kalamandalam Swarnadeepa has also undergone training in Drama & Recitation under Guru Itusree Mahanta, as well as Martial Arts, Folk and Creative Dance from 1995–2000.
